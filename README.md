# 🌿 MindEase: A Mental Health Support Platform 
> IP-Group-Project

### _A Calming and Supportive Website for Mental Wellness_

---

## 🧠 **Project Description**

**MindEase** is a simple yet thoughtful mental health support website designed to promote relaxation, positivity, and awareness of mental well-being. The project is for the **Internet Programming I** course at the **Addis Ababa Science and Technology University**.

The platform is being built using **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript** to provide accessible resources, guided exercises, motivational stories, and helpful tools that encourage users to prioritize self-care and mental health.

The goal of MindEase is to create a **safe and comforting digital space** that supports users through calm visuals and empowering messages—fostering a sense of peace and encouragement.
